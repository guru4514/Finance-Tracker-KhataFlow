Enterprise-Grade

Your financial data deserves bank-grade protection.

We take security seriously. Here's how we protect every transaction.

Encrypted Data

Powered by Google Firebase. All data is encrypted both in transit (TLS) and at rest on Google's secure infrastructure.

Local-First Storage

In Personal Mode, your data stays on your device in IndexedDB. Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly sync.

Role-Based Access

Agents see only their assigned customers. They cannot view other agents' data or the organization's total capital.

Approval Workflows

Every payment submitted by an agent must be approved by an Admin before it's recorded. No unauthorized changes.

Comprehensive Audit Logs

Every action — customer creation, payment edits, deletions — is permanently logged with timestamps and user IDs.

Firestore Security Rules

Server-side security rules validate every read and write. Even if someone tries to bypass the app, the database rejects invalid requests.

Your data is safe with us.
