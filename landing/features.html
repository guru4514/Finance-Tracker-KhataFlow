12 Powerful Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Pigmie is built specifically for the daily realities of pigmy agents and micro-finance teams in India.

Smart Collection Prompts

Auto-calculate collection splits between Principal and Interest with intelligent daily repayment suggestions.

Offline-First Personal Mode

Work seamlessly without internet. Data is cached locally in IndexedDB and syncs to the cloud when you're back online.

Real-Time Organization Mode

Manage collection agents with live cloud synchronization. See daily collections update in real time across your organization.

Customer Passbook Portal

Give customers zero-login access to view their live repayment passbook using their unique Customer ID. Learn more →

Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Assign specific roles: Owner, Admin, Manager, Agent, and Viewer with strict least-privilege permissions.

Approval Workflows

Agents submit collection requests for Admin approval, preventing fraud and accidental record modifications.

Multi-Language Support

Use the app in English, Kannada, or Hindi. Perfect for field agents across India's diverse linguistic regions.

Reports & Analytics

Collection trends, defaulter lists (3+ days overdue), agent performance cards — all auto-generated from your data.

Bulk Collection

Select multiple customers and collect from all of them at once. Perfect for agents with fixed daily routes.

Loan & Savings Dual Support

Manage both loan customers (collect repayments) and savings customers (collect deposits) in the same app.

Comprehensive Audit Log

Every customer addition, payment edit, and loan closure is permanently logged for full accountability and transparency.

Native Android & PWA

Run natively on Android devices via Capacitor or install as a Progressive Web App directly to your home screen.
